## 2. What the research proof (`../nikodym-fq/WRITEUP3.md`) depends on — lemma by lemma

The research exclusion of |C| = 19 (the only size that needs excluding, by heredity) runs:
hand lemmas ⇒ s = max secant ∈ {4,5,6}, 9 ≤ e = #empty lines ≤ 13, finite concurrence of empty lines ≤ 6,
parallel class ≤ 5, a frame of empty lines exists, **two empty lines are parallel** (Desargues step),
⇒ four normalised empty frames (rectangle; or three one-parallel-pair frames with "unique repeated
direction"); each frame is then closed by a custom "direct tree" certificate checked by the research's own
C++ checker (`check_closed.cpp`, unverified).  Every hand lemma was re-derived by me and the cheap ones were
recomputed with fresh code (`nik29/lemma_checks.py`, 10 checks, imports only `src/ff.py`, `src/ag.py`; all pass,
5.8 min).

### WLOG / symmetry claims used by the release split (all checked by `src/symmetry_check_nik29.py`, q = 7, 8, 9)
1. AGL(2,q) transitive on lines (explicit map for each of the 90 lines).
2. (x,y) ↦ (a x^σ + b, y^σ) are collineations fixing the axis setwise; the orbit representatives of
   s-subsets of the axis cover all C(9,s) subsets disjointly, every representative contains 0 and 1.
3. Every non-axis line through (0,0) is sent to x = 0 by a shear fixing the axis pointwise.
4. Every non-axis line through (1,0) is sent to x = 1 or x = 1 + y by (x,y) ↦ (x, dy), which fixes the
   axis pointwise and x = 0 setwise.
Every map is verified to map every line onto a line, so it preserves "Nikodym complement", |C| and the
maximum secant size.  Nothing needs a full stabiliser.

## 3. The release re-proof: a different, simpler split (max-secant), no hand lemma in the trust base

I did **not** reuse the research reduction. The release pipeline needs a split whose soundness is checked by
script, and whose cases are closed by CaDiCaL → lrat-trim → cake_lpr. Two candidates were piloted:

| split | hand content | pilot at q = 9, K = 19 |
|---|---|---|
| flag split of Nik(2,8) (`enc_nikodym.py`, |C| ≥ 19, cubes on the witness lines of (1,1),(2,2)) | none beyond `symmetry_check.py` (passes for q = 9) | cube 0 of flag_h: **timeout 900 s, LRAT 3.3 GB** — a depth-2 cube is already too hard; ~128 nontrivial cubes per flag case → not viable in the time available |
| **max-secant split** (`src/enc_nik29.py`, this report) | heredity (2 lines) + the 4 WLOG steps below, all checked by `src/symmetry_check_nik29.py` | s = 6 whole case: 55 s; s = 2,3,7,8,9: ≤ 93 s; s = 5: 1460 s (one case) or > 1800 s; s = 4: > 1200 s whole, 40–360 s per depth-2 cube |

**The split.** Let C be a Nikodym complement with |C| = 19 (exactly: by heredity any complement with ≥ 19
points contains one with 19). Let s = max_L |L ∩ C| (2 ≤ s ≤ 9) and A a line attaining it.
1. AGL(2,9) is transitive on lines: WLOG A = {y = 0} (explicit affine map exhibited for each of the 90 lines).
2. (x,y) ↦ (a x^σ + b, y^σ) fixes A setwise and acts on it as AΓL(1,9): WLOG A ∩ C = S, an orbit
   representative containing 0 and 1 (orbit sizes sum to C(9,s); representatives: s=2: 1, s=3: 2, s=4: 3,
   s=5: 3, s=6: 2, s=7,8,9: 1 each — 14 representatives).
3. (0,0) ∈ C has a free line T₀ ≠ A; a shear fixing A pointwise maps it to x = 0 (all 9 lines checked).
4. (1,0) ∈ C has a free line T₁ ≠ A; (x,y) ↦ (x, dy) fixes A pointwise and x = 0 setwise and maps T₁ to
   x = 1 (`tan=h`) or x = 1 + y (`tan=d`) (all 9 lines checked).
5. Every line has at most s points of C (preserved by every collineation).
Cases: 14 × 2 = **28 CNFs** (`specs/nik29.json`): c_p, w_{x,L}, witness clauses, |C| = 19 (sequential
counters both ways), at-most-s on every line, units for S, T₀, T₁. The s = 9 case makes the full-line lemma
unnecessary (it is UNSAT in 0.02 s), s = 2, 3 make the local tangent bound and §3 of the writeup
unnecessary (UNSAT in ≤ 93 s). So the only hand content is heredity and the four WLOG steps. For the hard
cases (s = 4; s = 5 except o0h) the witness clauses of the two axis points of S other than (0,0),(1,0) are
split into 10 × 10 cubes (`specs/nik29cubes_s4.json`, `specs/nik29cubes_s5.json`, 101 instances per
case: 100 cubes + a cover CNF "witness clauses ∧ ¬cube_i for all i", UNSAT ⇒ exhaustive, all covers
verified in < 0.1 s). A depth-3 pilot (third point (1,1)) gave the same total work as depth 2 (cubes 0–9
of depth 3: 42 s vs 43 s for their parent) with max 22 s per cube, so depth 3 is the fallback for any
depth-2 timeout (`nik29/refine.py` writes the refinement spec and checks parent ⊂ child literals).

**Encoder controls** (all with the new encoder, fresh CNFs, cake_lpr for every UNSAT, independent
`check_nikodym.py` for every SAT model):
* q = 3, 4, 5: all cases UNSAT at |C| = 5, 7, 9 and SAT at 4, 6, 8 with Nikodym-checked models — reproduces
  Nik(2,q) = 5, 10, 17 (`nik29/tiny` run, see §5 log).
* q = 7 (`specs/nik29ctrl7.json`): 16/16 cases UNSAT at |C| = 14 (cake_lpr), and at |C| = 13 exactly the
  s = 4, orbit 1 cases (h and d) are SAT with 13-point Nikodym-checked models — reproduces Nik(2,7) = 36.
* q = 9 SAT side (`specs/nik29sat.json`): |C| = 18, s = 6, orbit 0: SAT (h in 0.4 s, d in 0.4 s), both
  models decoded and accepted by `check_nikodym.py` (|N| = 63); orbit 1: UNSAT at 18 (verified) — consistent
  with the writeup's 3-row witness, whose rows miss an F₃-line, i.e. orbit 0 of 6-subsets.
